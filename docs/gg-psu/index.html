GG-PSU — documentation index

GZLOZONE / Zero Zone GG-PSU — dual high-voltage regulated channels + LT1085 filament supply. Feeds GG-PRE AMP: ±200 V and ~12 V heaters (external transformer required).
Marking ZERO ZONE · GG-PSU
PCB (seller) 130 × 70 mm
Outputs 2× regulated HV (strapped ±200 V), adjustable LV ~3.5–12.6 V
BOM status Frozen — component ID; CR1 zener P/N → use 200 V 5 W I

Safety: Mains-derived 400 V class capacitors. Bleed before service. Lethal voltages.

Building the kit: BUILD.md (trafo, PSU bring-up, ±200 V strap).
